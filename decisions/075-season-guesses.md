# 075 — Arvauspeli: season guesses: decisions

Feature #759, `specs/075-season-guesses.md`. Before a Veikkausliiga season's
first kickoff, a player guesses the champion, the six teams of
`Mestaruussarja`, the team in 12th place and the whole final table. Each is
paid for once, changed for free until the lock, and settled by the hourly run
when the season has answered it.

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| The rules are a module of their own, `guessing-game-season.ts`: the four kinds (`champion`, `upper`, `last`, `table`), their prices and rewards, the shape a guess must have, and the judging | S1, S2. Pure, so every figure is tested without a database |
| A season guess is a row of `game_season_guess`, migration `0028_create_game_season_guess`: the kind, and the TASO team ids in the guess's order, the table's first its 1st place. One per player, season and kind by `game_season_guess_identity_idx`. A change rewrites the teams and writes nothing to the ledger | S1, S3 |
| The ledger gains `season_guess_id` and three kinds: `season-guess` for the price, `season-reward` and `season-refund` for the settlement, one price and one settlement per season guess by two partial unique indexes. The balance and the leaderboard sum every kind, so a season guess counts in both with no change of theirs | S5 of specs/072's ledger, S8 |
| A guess is checked in the store, in one transaction with the player locked, as a match guess is: joined, a season stored, before the lock, its teams those of the season's stored `Runkosarja` fixtures, distinct and as many as the kind names, and for a first placement a balance that pays the price | S3, S9, the spec's Edge Cases |
| The lock is the season's first stored kickoff, read at the time of the request. Each guess stores the lock it was placed under (`lock_at`), and once the earliest stored lock has passed it is the lock in force if earlier than the first kickoff, so a postponed first match does not reopen it. A stored lock still ahead does not count: when the opener moves later before its kickoff, the lock moves with it, as S3 has it. A season nobody has guessed yet has no stored lock to keep, and nothing to protect. | S3, S15 |
| A change keeps `made_at` and writes `changed_at`, so both are kept, as the spec's data says | The spec's API & Data |
| The six are the regular season's top six by its table; a final place is a team's place in `Mestaruussarja` (group 2), or 6 + its place in `Karsintasarja` (group 3). The tables are read with `getSeasonStandings`, the standings page's own path, so a group shown from TASO's own numbers is judged by them | S4, S12 |
| Two indexes, `game_season_guess_season_idx` on season and kind, for the lock, the shares and the leaderboard, and `game_season_guess_open_idx` on the open rows, for the hourly run, both in `0028` | The reads go by season, and the hourly run reads only open rows |
| When a season's tables cannot be read, only a guess whose answer is due is named in the run's failures and retried; one not due waits, and once a later season is stored is voided and refunded | S16: a season replaced before it answered a guess is one not completed, whether or not its tables can be read |
| A group's answer is due once it has as many `FINISHED` matches as today's format gives it (`GROUP_MATCHES`: 132, 30, 15), a forfeited match included, so a schedule stored in part never settles early, and a match abandoned and replayed under a new id still lets it settle. A group with a match abandoned and never replayed waits, and S16 voids its guesses with the next season. The tables are read only when an open guess needs a part that is due | S4, S14, S16. Deciding whether an abandoned match will be replayed is a guess; waiting is not. Reading only what is needed spares the standings path, and TASO, an hourly read through the split |
| Settling runs in the hourly run right after the match guesses, one season and one guess at a time, each guess in its own transaction: the player locked first, as a match's settling and an account's deletion lock it, then the guess row, still open, so a racing run settles nothing twice and neither waits on a deletion in the other order. A season whose tables cannot be read names its open guesses in the run's failures and tries again next hour | S4, the spec's Logging |
| A guess still open once a later season's first fixture is stored is voided and its price refunded, `Kausi jäi kesken, pelimerkit palautettu` | S16 |
| The run logs its own line every hour, `Arvauspeli season guesses settled`, with `settled`, `rewarded`, `voided` and `failed`, beside specs/072's line for the match guesses. The spec had the counts in that line; settling a season is a step of its own, and one line each keeps the store's two halves apart | Changed in implementation; the spec now says so |
| `Kauden arvaukset` sits above `Tulevat ottelut`, open. Before the lock a form per kind, each with its own button text, `Arvaa mestari` and so on, `Muuta mestaria` once placed. After the lock the kinds the player guessed, and the shares of the champion and last-place picks whether the player guessed those kinds or not, to any joined player: the three most picked teams, then `muut`, from 3 guesses | S6, S7, and #759's criterion that the section shows the shares after the lock |
| Between seasons, the season just finished shows its section too, read-only, when the player has a guess in it; and once the next season's fixtures are stored, until its lock, under `Kauden {vuosi} arvaukset` beside the new season's | The view turns to `between` in the same run that settles the season's last match and its guesses, so without it a settled season guess would never be seen; and the next season's fixtures, stored in winter, would otherwise take its results away months before the next lock |
| A season guess is a guess on the leaderboard: a player with only season guesses is on it, and a settled season guess counts in `Ratkaistut`. Specs/073 S4 lists players "with at least one guess in the season", and its record is not edited | Miikka, 2026-11-06, "either is fine for me, surprise me!". Season guesses are made before round 1, when the board would otherwise be empty |
| A failed read of the section logs `Reading Arvauspeli season guesses failed` and leaves it out, not the game | As specs/073's shares |
| No rarity bonus on a season guess, and no model plays against one | S5, S10 |
| Tests: unit for the rules, the store, the action, the form, the page and the run; integration against Postgres for placing, the lock and settling once; end to end in `guessing-game-season.spec.ts` | Spec, Tests Required |
