# 865 — The active season is the newest with matches: decisions

Bug #865. TASO started listing the 2027 seasons in November 2026, months before any 2027 fixture. The domestic pages treated the discovered season, 2027, as the one in progress. So by the code every analytics panel and forecast tied to "the active season" left the 2026 pages, production's too, and the release run found `Kauden ennuste` and `Cupin ennuste` gone; the surprises, goals per game and table movement take the same season. On an empty database, the release run's, the pages also opened on 2027 with no table rows. Spec: `specs/011-current-season-discovery.md`, whose rule is that a page never lands on a season with no matches. Narrows `decisions/011-current-season-discovery.md`, where the discovered season was both the selector's ceiling and the season `needsRefresh` and the analytics treat as current.

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **A domestic page's active season is its default season**: the newest with matches, played or scheduled. The discovered season stays the selector's ceiling and nothing else | Miikka's call on #865: the analytics follow the newest season with matches, so 2026 until the first 2027 match is scheduled. Scheduled counts because Arvauspeli needs the new season before its kickoff, and 011's probe already counts an all-fixtures season as having matches |
| **Every argument the pages passed the discovered season now takes the active one**, refreshing included | `needsRefresh` refreshes any season at or above the one it is given. Given 2026, it still refreshes 2027, and 2026 again, as it did before TASO listed 2027. Splitting the two would have meant a second season threaded through every loader for no difference |
| **The fallback is the newest stored season, but never older than the season before the empty one**, clamped to the competition's range as before | The fallback was the newest stored season or, with none, the discovered season itself: the empty one the probe had just rejected. A part-filled database, such as a release run's after it has visited only older seasons of a cup, would land on the newest of those, below 2026. Production never saw either, because it has 2026 stored |
| **The cup-domestic e2e names only a cup's first season**, and expects every year from the newest offered down to it | The newest is whatever TASO lists that day. The floor is the part the tests were written for |
| **`cup-forecast.spec.ts` and `headings-and-spacing.spec.ts` keep their seeded and named 2026** | With this fix, 2026 is the active season until 2027 has a match, so they pass again. Once 2027 fixtures are published, they need the year moved, as every year-named e2e does at a season's turn |
