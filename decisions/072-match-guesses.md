# 072 — Arvauspeli: match guesses and the token balance: decisions

Feature #756, `specs/072-match-guesses.md`. A signed-in reader joins
`Arvauspeli` at `/arvauspeli`, gets a season's tokens, and guesses
Veikkausliiga matches, a 1X2 or an exact score. The hourly `predictions` run
settles them. Matches that do not go as listed are specs/076, released with
this one.

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| Three tables, migration `0021_create_guessing_game`. `game_player`: the user as its key, cascading on delete, and `joined_at`. `game_guess`: one row per player and TASO match id (unique), the kind, the 1X2 or both goal counts, `free`, `made_at`, and `settled_at` and `tier` once settled. `game_ledger`: append-only, the kind (`grant`, `guess`, `change`, `reward`), a signed `amount`, and the guess it belongs to | The spec's storage. A guess and the ledger cascade from the player, and the player from the user, so deleting the account deletes all of it |
| **The balance is the ledger's sum** for the player and season, never a stored figure. A cost is a negative amount; the floor's free guess writes a `guess` row of `0`, so every guess has its row | The spec's API & Data. A stored balance would be a second source of truth beside the rows that make it |
| Two partial unique indexes: one `grant` per player and season, and one `reward` per guess | A retried or racing write fails at the database, so a grant or a reward can never be paid twice whatever the code above it does |
| A guess is written in one transaction that first locks the player's `game_player` row (`select … for update`), then reads the match, the balance and the player's guesses, decides, and writes the guess with its ledger row | Two guesses sent at once are decided one after the other, so they never spend the same tokens. The integration test sends two with tokens for one; without the lock both were paid |
| A change rewrites the guess row and adds a `change` row of the current price. What the earlier version said is not kept, only what it cost | S4. Settling reads the guess as it stands at kickoff |
| The same guess sent again writes nothing and costs nothing. The form disables its button until the guess differs | Not in the spec. A change that changes nothing has nothing to pay for |
| The game's season is the newest Veikkausliiga season stored, `category_id` `VL` and that season's `spljpNN`. It is open while any of its matches is still to kick off or has no result. Then the page says `Kauden {next} ottelut julkaistaan myöhemmin.`, and with no season stored at all, `Veikkausliigan ottelut julkaistaan myöhemmin.` | S23. The page reads stored rows only; a new season's fixtures arrive as for every other page. The second string is new: with nothing stored there is no year to name |
| Every group of that season can be guessed: `Runkosarja`, `Mestaruussarja` and `Karsintasarja` in 2025 and 2026 | S10. The relegation play-off is not stored under `VL`, so nothing has to leave it out |
| The regular season is group `1`; any other group stored for the season means the split is stored. Before it, the previous season's matches outside group `1` are added to both the remaining and the total of S14's share | S14. Group `1` is the regular season in every Veikkausliiga season (specs/009). 2025 and 2026 each have 45 split matches |
| A season's grant is written at joining when a season is open, and otherwise when the player first opens the page or guesses in it. Its amount is decided from `joined_at`: the whole balance before the season's first kickoff, else S14's share at that moment | S2, S21 and S23 in one rule. A player who joined between seasons, or in an earlier one, joined before the first kickoff and gets 1 000. Writing on the page's read is the only way to give a returning player the balance "with no action of their own" |
| A reader who has not joined sees what joining would give now, and a line saying so once the season has started | The spec's join string |
| Prices by milliseconds before the stored kickoff: 10 at 168 h or more, 15 at 72 h or more, 20 at 24 h or more, 30 under, and locked at kickoff and after. The page shows `Hinta nyt {price}, nousee {next}:een {date klo time}` while it will rise, or `Muutos maksaa {price}` for a change | S3, S13. Every boundary belongs to the cheaper side. The spec's two strings joined into one line, with the date as the match page writes it (`formatKickoff`) |
| A row where only the floor's free 1X2 is open says `Maksuton 1X2-arvaus` and offers no score; the page's notice above the list says why. A free guess shows `Maksutonta arvausta ei voi muuttaa.` and no form, and the same sentence is the refusal if a change is sent anyway | S15. Showing a price of 10 on a row that is free would be false. Both strings are new (S20) |
| The floor is open on a match with no guess of the player's when their balance is below its price and no guess of theirs waits to be settled, a past match's included | S15's "no open guess" |
| Goal counts are whole numbers from 0 to 99, in the form and on the server | Two digits, a parser's bound (`skills/self-review.md`, section 6). `MAX_GUESSED_GOALS` is the one rule both read |
| Settling: after the hourly run writes its predictions, every open guess whose match is `FINISHED` with both scores is judged, one match per transaction. The guess is updated only while `settled_at` is null, and its `reward` row, `0` included, is written in the same transaction | S6, S9, S11. With the update's guard and the open-guess filter both removed, the integration test still found a second payment, and the reward index stopped it |
| A match whose guesses fail to settle logs `Unable to settle Arvauspeli guesses` at `error` with its TASO match id, adds `settle match {id}` to the run's failures, and the run goes on with the next match. A step that fails outright adds `settle` | The spec's Logging. A failure makes the Railway run exit 1, as a failed refresh does |
| `npm run predictions -- settle` runs the step alone and prints `Settled {n} guess(es)` and each failure | S11, for staging, which has no cron |
| The hourly run refreshes Veikkausliiga's matches 48 hours ahead and 24 hours after kickoff, as for its predictions. A kickoff moved further ahead is seen when a page refreshes the season | The lock is the stored kickoff (S3), and the run keeps it current through the last 48 hours before it. Kickoff moves are specs/076's |
| A guess on a past match that has no result is listed under `Odottavat tulosta`, and so is a settled guess whose match has since lost its result | The spec's "their open guesses". The heading is new (S20) |
| `Ratkaistut` lists the settled guesses newest first: the result, the guess, and `Oikea tulos +100`, `Oikea maaliero +40`, `Oikea merkki +{n}` or `Ei osunut` | The spec's strings |
| The page's three sections fold by their `h2`, a count after it: `Tulevat ottelut (3 ottelua)` starts open, `Odottavat tulosta (1 arvaus)` and `Ratkaistut (12 arvausta)` closed. Nothing is remembered | `skills/ui-review.md`, classes 2 and 3: only a page's most basic part starts open, and `Ratkaistut` grows to the season's 177 matches. Remembering a choice would add a kind of page to `section-keys.ts`, which the spec does not ask for |
| `Tulevat ottelut` is grouped by the day in Helsinki, as `la 10.4.2027`, with `klo 18.00` on each row, and each row names its group only when the list holds more than one. An empty list says `Ei tulevia otteluita.` | The spec's UX. The empty line is new (S20) |
| The form: a radio group of `1`, `X`, `2` and `Arvaa tulos`, two number fields labelled `{team}, maalit` for a score, a hidden legend `Arvaus: {home} – {away}`, and `Arvaa` or `Muuta arvausta`. A refusal shows under the row: `Ottelu on jo alkanut.`, `Pelimerkit eivät riitä.`, `Maksutonta arvausta ei voi muuttaa.`, or `Arvausta ei voitu tallentaa. Yritä uudelleen.` for anything else. After a success the page refreshes, which reads the new balance on the server | The spec's strings. Each radio and field is named for a screen reader, since a page has a form per match |
| A failed join says `Liittyminen epäonnistui. Yritä uudelleen.`; a page whose session or game cannot be read says `Arvauspelin lataaminen epäonnistui. Yritä myöhemmin uudelleen.` and logs `Reading Arvauspeli failed` | New strings (S20), shaped as `/suosikit`'s. A failure is never shown as "signed out" (`skills/self-review.md`, section 5) |
| Log lines, ids only: `Arvauspeli joined` (user), `Arvauspeli grant written` (user, season, amount), `Arvauspeli guess written` (user, TASO match id, kind, `new` / `change` / `free` / `unchanged`, price), `Arvauspeli guess refused` (the same and the reason), `Arvauspeli guesses settled` (matches, rewarded, missed, failed), all `info`. A request with no session or a malformed guess logs at `warn`, a failed action at `error` | The spec's Logging |
| `/arvauspeli` maps to `src/app/guessing-game`, is rendered per request, and is the front page's fifth tile, after `Ennusteet` | S12. Finnish URLs, English code (decisions/012) |
| `/tietosuoja` lists the game's data, says the game is a purpose its data is kept for ("jotta voit pelata arvauspeliä"), and that deleting the account removes it | Every per-user table is on the page, and its purpose |
| Tests: unit for the rules at every boundary, the store's branches over a faked client, the actions' refusals, the two components and the route. Integration for joining and the grant, a guess, a change, the floor, two guesses at once, settling once, and the cascade. End to end in `guessing-game.spec.ts` | Spec, Tests Required |
