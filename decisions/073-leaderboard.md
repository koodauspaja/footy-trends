# 073 — Arvauspeli: leaderboard: decisions

Feature #757, `specs/073-leaderboard.md`. `/arvauspeli` shows signed-in readers
a season's leaderboard ranked by balance, under each player's nickname
(specs/077); the shares of the players' guesses on a match once it has kicked
off; and a rarity bonus on a correct 1X2 few others made.

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| The board and the shares are read in `guessing-game-board.ts`, beside `openGame` and after it, not inside its snapshot. Each fails on its own: a failed board says `Tulostaulukon lataaminen epäonnistui. Yritä myöhemmin uudelleen.` in its section and logs `Reading the Arvauspeli leaderboard failed`; failed shares are left out and log `Reading Arvauspeli guess shares failed` | Reading other players decides no write, so it needs no lock or shared snapshot with the player's own rows. Neither is the game: a failure of either must not take the page with it, as specs/077's nickname read was made not to. The section's line is new (S8) |
| The board's season is the one the view shows: the season in play, or between seasons the one just finished; before any season is stored there is no board | The spec's "between seasons" edge case. `between` names the next season, so the board's is the one before it |
| The board lists every player with a guess in the season, from `game_guess`, with their settled count and nickname, beside the season's ledger sums, in one read-only repeatable-read transaction. A player with a guess and no ledger row is ranked at 0 | S4. One snapshot, so a balance and its settled count describe the same moment |
| Ranks by balance, highest first. An equal balance shares a rank and the next counts every player above it (1, 1, 3). In a tie, a nickname comes before none, by Finnish collation, and the user id keeps the order stable | The spec's edge case. Finnish collation puts `Ä` after `Z`, as a Finnish reader expects |
| The page gets the rank, nickname, balance, settled count, the row's place in the order and whether it is the reader's own, never a user id. The first 50 are shown, and the reader's row after a `…` row when it is further down | Spec, UX. The place keys the row, since two players without a nickname may share a rank |
| `Tulostaulukko` is a table, `Sija`, `Pelaaja`, `Pelimerkit`, `Ratkaistut`, folding by its `h2` with `(n pelaajaa)`, starting open, after `Tulevat ottelut`. The reader's row is bold, with `(sinä)` after the name. A reader who has not joined sees it under the way in; between seasons, under the season line | The spec's strings, as a table so each figure has its column: `{n} ratkaistua` became the column `Ratkaistut`. `(sinä)` says what the bold shows, for a reader who cannot see bold (S8) |
| The shares: per match among the reader's waiting and settled guesses that have kicked off, every guess on it counted by its 1X2, a score guess's by its goals, computed in SQL. Counted: a guess still open made before the stored kickoff, and a settled one with a played or awarded settlement. Shown from 3 guesses, as rounded per cents, `Pelaajien arvaukset: 1 {a} % · X {b} % · 2 {c} % ({n} arvausta)` | S6. A guess made at or after the kickoff will be void, and a refunded one never counted, as the spec says. Rounded per cents may sum to 99 or 101 |
| The bonus: in the settling transaction of each match, after its rewards, among the guesses that transaction settled. With 5 or more settled as played (`exact`, `difference`, `outcome`, `miss`), and at most a quarter of them with the 1X2 right, each right one gets a `bonus` ledger row of 20 | S7. Counting only what the transaction settled means a guess another run settled first never makes this run pay a second bonus, and a void, refunded or awarded guess neither counts nor earns |
| A partial unique index, `game_ledger_bonus_idx`, allows one `bonus` row per guess, migration `0025_add_game_bonus_index` | As 072's settlement index: a retried or racing write fails at the database |
| The run's summary counts `bonuses`; the printed `Settled n guess(es)` is unchanged | The spec's Logging. A bonus is not a guess |
| `Ratkaistut` shows `Harvinainen osuma +20` after the tier's line, and the shares after it | Spec, UX |
| The privacy page's game line adds that other signed-in users see the player's balance on the leaderboard | The spec's privacy line. specs/077 already says they see the nickname |
| Tests: unit for the rules at every boundary, the reads over a faked client, the view's bonus, settling's bonus, the page and the route; integration for the board, the shares and the bonus against Postgres; end to end in `guessing-game.spec.ts` | Spec, Tests Required |
